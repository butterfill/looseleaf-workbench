# Source checklist — second-language pronunciation training

[Main entry](../../entries/second-language-pronunciation-training.md)

**Status (11 October 2026):** The four previously publisher-verified meta-analyses are now available in **Research / Personal Papers as original full texts**, with the verified keys below. The conceptual entry has been rewritten in light of their methods, results and discussions. This is a focused synthesis, not a systematic review of all primary L2 training experiments or a full reanalysis of appendices. Overlapping studies appear across the reviews: do not add study counts or treat the four summaries as independent replications.

| Source | What was consulted | Note and unresolved limitation |
|---|---|---|
| \citet{saito:2019_effects} | Original full text, framework, method, results, discussion and conclusion | [Source note](saito2019_effects.md); no independent supporting-appendix audit; controlled versus spontaneous production |
| \citet{uchihara:2025_high} | Original full text, HVPT procedure, pooled and moderator analyses, retention, generalisation and discussion | [Source note](uchihara2025_high.md); coding and supplementary analyses not re-run; predominantly perceptual outcomes |
| \citet{uchihara:2024_does} | Original full text, inclusion criteria, production effects, transfer, retention, moderators and discussion | [Source note](uchihara2024_does.md); supplemental data not independently re-analysed; mostly controlled production tests |
| \citet{yao:2025_metaanalysis} | Original full text, quality procedure, moderator comparisons, generalisation, discussion and limitations | [Source note](yao2025_metaanalysis.md); subgroup and dependence questions require independent statistical audit |

**Citation provenance:** Research returns the listed keys and CSL-JSON bibliographic metadata for all four papers. Its BibTeX export for at least \citet{saito:2019_effects} returned “entry not found” at the time of this update; the record key is verified, but a successful BibTeX export has not been established. ## Still to investigate

- [x] Locate original full texts and verified keys for the four leading meta-analyses, rather than relying on publisher abstracts.
- [x] Distinguish improvements in phoneme identification/discrimination from production, transfer, retention and spontaneous use.
- [x] Record numerical/methodological specifics separately from the main conceptual entry.
- [ ] Read and appraise matched **primary comparisons** of perception-only training, production practice, explicit articulatory information and combined interventions. Candidate studies named within the reviews include Herd et al. (2013), Kartushina et al. (2015), Cibelli (2022), and Chang (2023); no Personal Papers full-text access to these primary studies was established in this pass.
- [ ] Audit original trials with articulatory measures (ultrasound, tongue motion or other articulography), rather than inferring gestures from acoustic outcomes.
- [ ] Scrutinise delayed, generalised and spontaneous production under meaningful communicative demands; assess reliability and risk of bias in primary studies and supplementary analyses.
- [ ] Look for changes in gesture- or action-event experience that survive controls for acoustic discriminability and explicit phoneme labels, and then test their possible relation to deliberate action selection.
- [ ] Verify whether the four Research keys are available to the downstream BibTeX citation workflow, rather than inferring this from metadata presence.
