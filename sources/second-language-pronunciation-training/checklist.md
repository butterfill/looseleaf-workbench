## Major publisher-verified meta-analyses (11 October 2026)

- Saito & Plonsky (2019), *Language Learning* 69:652–708, DOI 10.1111/lang.12345: 77 studies, outcomes separated by specificity, monitored versus spontaneous production. Publisher abstract and article bibliography checked; full statistics not independently audited. **Not in Personal Papers**.
- Uchihara et al. (2024), *Applied Psycholinguistics* 45:591–623, DOI 10.1017/S0142716424000195: 31 HVPT studies, trained/untrained production and retention; original publisher article text (abstract, introduction, methodology discussion) consulted. **Not in Personal Papers**.
- Uchihara, Karas & Thomson (2025), *Studies in Second Language Acquisition*: 79 HVPT perception studies; g=0.92 pre/post, g=0.67 treatment/control; original publisher article text consulted. **Not in Personal Papers**.
- Yao et al. (2025), *JSLHR* 68:1784–1802, DOI 10.1044/2024_JSLHR-24-00432: 65 studies, N=2793, 223 effect sizes, d=0.762; original publisher abstract and references checked. **Not in Personal Papers**.

These are **overlapping evidence bases**, not four independent replications. Do not add their study counts or combine effect sizes. Need bias assessment, moderator confidence intervals, and full supplementary tables before claims about superiority of training approaches.
