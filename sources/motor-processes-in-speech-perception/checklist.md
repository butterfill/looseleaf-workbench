## Questions to resolve before promotion

- [ ] Establish which **specific computations** are supported: phoneme manipulation/maintenance, acoustic prediction, articulatory-cause estimation, sensorimotor coupling, uncertainty-dependent compensation or executive decision processes. Separate supported claims from speculative mechanisms.
- [ ] Cross task type (implicit discrimination, explicit identification, semantic comprehension), SNR, linguistic familiarity and articulatory expertise; compare sensitivity separately from response criteria.
- [ ] Directly compare acoustic, phoneme-identity, articulatory-feature and production-to-listening cross-decoding models on the same stimuli and neural measures. Check where published positive and null results actually target the same hypothesis.
- [ ] Reconcile the anatomical findings of \citet{du:2014_noise}, \citet{perron:2024_left} and \citet{perron:2024_engagement} without collapsing primary motor, premotor, frontal operculum and inferior-frontal regions.
- [ ] Acquire independent replications and full methodological audits for causal TMS and articulatory restriction experiments; complete source notes for the original studies still marked pending above.
- [ ] Clarify the distinction between contributions to *phoneme judgments*, to acoustic **sensitivity**, to speech **comprehension**, and to **experience of articulatory events**. The last is the principal question of the [gesture-recovery companion](../../entries/phonetic-gestures-and-speech-perception.md).
