## Corrections made in the 14 October revision

- Replaced the DOI hyperlink for the 2024 vowel-generalisation study with its verified Personal Papers citation key, \citet{parrell:2024_sensorimotor}.
- Removed two named studies that could not be matched to Personal Papers records for original-text checking (Houde & Jordan 1998; Jones & Munhall 2000). Their details are recorded below for possible later acquisition, but their results are **not** attributed as independently verified evidence in the current entry.
- Corrected the category-boundary description: the main Near/Far analysis in \citet{niziolek:2013_vowel} used post hoc groups of naturally varying productions; 18 participated in imaging, but the final imaging analysis included 16.
- Clarified that \citet{parrell:2024_sensorimotor} found group-level generalisation to five untrained vowels, while not every individual vowel showed a statistically reliable change. Twenty-two were recruited and 21 analysed (one equipment-related exclusion).
- Clarified that the all-participants-adapted statement in \citet{lametti:2012_sensory} applies to the 47 speakers in the three conditions receiving both perturbations, and that \citet{parrell:2026_audiomotor} did not directly exclude subthreshold muscle activity or silent articulatory movements on successfully inhibited trials.
- Renamed the lexical section to reflect **word/nonword status**, rather than implying that the experiment manipulated word meaning.

## Outstanding coverage limits

The entry introduces major selected phenomena but is not exhaustive: systematic searches for replications, counterexamples, pitch-specific mechanisms, developmental populations, clinical populations, and connected spontaneous speech have not been completed. The text should not be interpreted as establishing a unique computational architecture. Original full texts were consulted for the eight cited works below, but supplementary materials and replication literatures have not been exhaustively reviewed. Source-specific notes beyond the existing FACTS note have not yet been prepared.
