## Outstanding research

- [ ] Systematic literature search for altered pitch, formant and timing feedback; include negative results, response variability and replications.
- [ ] Distinguish within-utterance compensation from trial-to-trial recalibration and perceptual retuning in each design.
- [ ] Read complete results and supplementary materials of each original experiment and compare model predictions.
- [ ] Test whether manipulation of *intention content*, holding lexical target and motor preparation constant, is feasible.
- [ ] Assess developmental and clinical generalisability; do not infer from small laboratory vowel tasks to spontaneous speech.
