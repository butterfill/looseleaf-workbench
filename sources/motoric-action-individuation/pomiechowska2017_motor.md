# \citet{pomiechowska:2017_motor}: motor recruitment may follow action interpretation

Pomiechowska, Barbara & Gergely Csibra (2017), “Motor Activation during Action Perception Depends on Action Interpretation,” *Neuropsychologia* 105, 84–91. DOI: 10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2017.01.032. Verified Personal Papers key: `pomiechowska:2017_motor`.

**Access:** Original Personal Papers full text, abstract, theoretical framing, EEG experiments/results and Discussion.

**Experiment/findings.** Participants viewed grasping, reaching and pointing following speech or other sound, while EEG mu suppression indexed sensorimotor recruitment. Mu suppression differed with the apparent instrumental versus communicative construal of an observed movement, notably for grasping; interpretation may condition motor activation.

**Relevance.** This is a useful countermodel to the inference from action-related motor activation to motoric structuring of the action represented. The causal arrow might be from conceptual interpretation to motor response, rather than the reverse.

**Caveats.** Mu suppression is an indirect, disputed indicator of motor simulation; the samples are relatively small and no direct action-individuation task was used. The findings complicate an overstrong motor-first theory rather than disproving a more specific role of motoric organization.
